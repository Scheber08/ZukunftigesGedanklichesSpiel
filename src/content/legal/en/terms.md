## 1. What this is about

[LIGANAME] ([KÜRZEL]) is a private, non-commercial online league for EA SPORTS F1® 25. We are an unofficial fan league and are not affiliated with Formula One Group, the FIA, Electronic Arts or Codemasters.

These terms set out what applies to your participation – in particular which data we publish and how we handle your clips. What applies on track and on Discord is set out in the [rulebook](/en/league/rules). How we handle your data is explained in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).

Taking part is **free of charge**.

## 2. Who can take part

- You must be **at least 16 years old**. You confirm this when you sign up.
- Only one sign-up per person is allowed. You race yourself, using your own account.
- Your details must be correct. False information, for example about your age, leads to exclusion.
- League management decides who is accepted and who gets a regular or reserve place.

## 3. What we publish about you

For a league to work, results and decisions must be visible to everyone. We therefore publish on the website and in our channels:

- your **gamertag**,
- your **race number** (including previous numbers in the history),
- your **platform** (PC, PlayStation or Xbox),
- an optional **flag**,
- your team and your role (regular or reserve driver),
- your **results**, lap times, positions in the standings and statistics,
- **steward decisions** you are involved in – with description, clip and reasoning.

We do not publish your Discord name, EA ID or other private details.

## 4. Your clips

When you report an incident, you submit a link to a clip.

- You confirm that you recorded the clip yourself or are allowed to use it.
- You allow us to link to or embed the clip in the public steward decision, free of charge and without time limit. This right is non-exclusive – you can continue to use the clip yourself.
- We only use your clip for other purposes, such as highlights or social media, if you have agreed beforehand.
- If you delete the clip at the provider, it can no longer be played on our site either. Please therefore keep it online at least until the end of the season.

The game content in the clip belongs to the respective rights holders. We use it exclusively on a non-commercial basis within the league. **[CHECK: EA's guidelines on videos containing game content]**

## 5. Streams and broadcasts

We plan to broadcast races live or publish recordings at a later stage.

- Before we start, we will inform all drivers in good time and – where necessary – obtain your consent. **[CHECK: form of consent]**
- Your gamertag, race number, car and driving may be visible in a broadcast.
- We will **only broadcast or publish your voice** (e.g. from the Discord voice channel) **if you have expressly agreed beforehand** (opt-in). You can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future.
- You may stream your own races. Please respect other people's privacy: only include voice chats if everyone agrees, and do not show other people's private data.

## 6. Conduct

The [rulebook](/en/league/rules), including its rules on conduct on Discord, applies. In short: race fair, stay respectful, no cheats, no insults, no discrimination.

## 7. No entitlement to participate

- There is no entitlement to be accepted, to a regular seat, a particular team or a particular race number.
- Races may be postponed or cancelled, for example due to technical problems or too few participants.
- We do our best to keep the website and our services available. There is no guarantee of this.

## 8. Exclusion

League management may suspend you temporarily or exclude you from the league if you seriously or repeatedly breach the rulebook or these terms – for example by cheating, insulting others, discriminating against others or giving false information. As a rule, you will receive a warning first. In serious cases, exclusion may be immediate.

## 9. Leaving, deletion and pseudonymisation

- You can leave the league at any time. A short message to league management is enough.
- Your results are kept so that standings and statistics remain correct for everyone else.
- On request, we replace your gamertag with a pseudonym (e.g. "Former driver") and delete your private contact details. Details are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).
- Your race number may then be given to other drivers.

- We are not responsible for outages or errors of the game, platforms, Discord or your internet connection.
- The providers of external sites we link to (e.g. clips on YouTube or Twitch) are responsible for their content.

We may change these terms, for example when new features are added. We will announce significant changes on the website and on Discord at least two weeks in advance. If a change requires your consent, we will ask you first. If you do not agree with a change, you can leave the league at any time.
