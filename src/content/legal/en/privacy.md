## The key points at a glance

- We are a private hobby league for EA SPORTS F1® 25. We only collect the data we need to run the league.
- Only your **gamertag**, **race number**, **platform**, an optional **flag**, your results and any steward decisions you were involved in are public.

## 2. When you visit our website: hosting, CDN and server logs

Our website is hosted by **Cloudflare, Inc.**, 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare delivers the pages through a worldwide network of servers (CDN) and protects them against attacks.

When you open a page, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data, for example:

- your IP address,
- the date and time of the request,
- the page requested and the page you came from,
- browser, operating system and device type (user agent),
- the status of the request (e.g. "successful" or "error").

**Purpose:** we need this data to deliver the website, find errors and fend off attacks.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is a secure, working website.

**Retention:** Cloudflare only keeps these logs for a short time. We do not analyse them in a way that identifies individuals. **[CHECK: enter retention period according to Cloudflare configuration and contract]**

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Cloudflare (Art. 28 GDPR). For transfers to the USA, see section 15.

## 3. Protecting our forms: Cloudflare Turnstile and abuse protection

We protect our forms (sign-up, incident report, contact) against spam and bots with **Cloudflare Turnstile**. Turnstile checks in the background whether a human is using the form. To do this, Cloudflare processes, among other things, your IP address and technical characteristics of your browser and device. You usually do not need to solve a puzzle.

In addition, when you submit a form we store a **hash of your IP address**. This is an encrypted string from which the IP address cannot easily be recovered. We use it to limit how often someone can submit a form within a short time.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is protection against spam and abuse. Where Turnstile accesses information on your device, this is strictly necessary to protect the form you are using (Section 25(2) No. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, TDDDG). **[CHECK]**

**Retention:** IP hashes are deleted automatically after **30 days**.

## 6. Signing up as a driver

When you sign up at [/en/join](/en/join), we process:

- gamertag, Discord name and platform,
- input device (wheel or controller),
- preferred race number, preferred role (regular or reserve driver) and your availability,
- optionally: EA ID, nationality (for the flag), experience and a reference lap time,
- your confirmations (minimum age 16, rulebook read) with time stamp and rulebook version,
- a hash of your IP address (section 3).

We do not ask for your date of birth.

**Purpose:** we decide whether to accept you, organise your participation and contact you via Discord.

**Forwarding to Discord:** after you submit the form, we send a short notification via a so-called webhook to a non-public staff channel on our Discord server. It contains only the essentials (e.g. gamertag, Discord name, platform, preferred race number). More about Discord in section 9.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (preparing and carrying out your participation). Showing a flag is optional. It is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

**Retention:**

- If you are not accepted, we delete your sign-up **6 months** after it was processed.
- If you are accepted, we keep your data for as long as you are in the league. For leaving the league, see section 13.
- IP hashes are deleted after **30 days**.

## 7. Reporting an incident to the stewards

When you report an incident at [/en/stewards/report](/en/stewards/report), we process:

- round and session, drivers involved, lap and corner,
- your description,
- the link to your clip and the timestamp,
- your Discord name for follow-up questions,
- a hash of your IP address.

**Purpose:** the stewards assess the incident under the rulebook.

**Publication:** we publish the stewards' decision on the website – with a reference number, the gamertags of the drivers involved, the description, the clip and the reasoning. We do not publish your Discord name for follow-up questions. The clip may be embedded. It only loads after you click (section 11).

**Forwarding to Discord:** a short notification containing only the essentials (e.g. round and reference) is sent via webhook to a non-public staff channel.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (running the league according to the rulebook and the [terms of participation](/en/terms)). For publishing decisions, also Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is transparent and fair decisions.

**Retention:** we delete your Discord name for follow-up questions at the **end of the season**. IP hashes are deleted after **30 days**. Decisions remain published as part of the league's history (section 13).

## 10. Web analytics with Google Analytics 4 – only with your consent

If you consent in the cookie banner, we use **Google Analytics 4**, a service provided by **Google Ireland Limited**, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It shows us which pages are used and how often, so we can improve the website.

**Without your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded.** We use Consent Mode v2 in its "basic" variant: until you consent, no connection to Google is established and no data is sent to Google.

With your consent, Google processes, for example:

- pages viewed, time and duration of the visit,
- the page you came from,
- information about your device, browser and screen,
- approximate location (country, region), derived from your IP address,
- a random identifier in a cookie to recognise returning visits.

**Our settings:**

- According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. The IP address is only used briefly to determine your approximate location and is then discarded.
- Google Signals is switched off.
- Use for advertising and personalised ads is switched off.
- Analytics data is retained for **2 months**.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future – via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of every page.

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (section 15).

## 17. Your rights

- **rectification** of incorrect data (Art. 16 GDPR),
